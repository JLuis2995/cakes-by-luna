Baked to order, decorated by hand

Cakes by Luna is a custom bakery in Ocala, Florida. Every order starts with a conversation: the occasion, the theme, the colors, how many guests are coming.

From there, each cake is baked for your date and decorated by hand. Piped buttercream flowers, spiral roses, little modeled figures like teddy bears and witch hats, edible paper butterflies. We work in English and Spanish.
